Legal Rights & Protection Acts

Clear, accessible breakdowns of critical Indian legal provisions and citizen rights during crisis situations.

  • Zero-FIR: File complaint at any police station regardless of jurisdiction
  • POCSO Act: Strict protection for minors against offenses
  • POSH Act: Workplace harassment prevention and IC guidelines
  • Section 46(4) CrPC: Women arrest restrictions during sunset to sunrise

Situational Defense & Commute Safety

Practical safety habits, de-escalation strategies, and tactical transit protocols for daily navigation.

  • Safe Transit Rules: Verify cab license & child locks before entry
  • Vulnerable Navigation: Stay near lighted paths & active storefronts
  • De-escalation: Firm verbal boundaries and spatial defense
  • Digital Discretion: Keep emergency contacts on speed-dial

First-Aid & Crisis Handling

Essential immediate medical protocols to stabilize emergencies before professional responders arrive.

  • Severe Bleeding: Direct firm pressure and elevation
  • Unconscious Person: Recovery position & airway clearance
  • Accident Protocol: Call 112, do not move suspected spinal injury
  • Panic Attack Management: 4-7-8 breathing & tactile grounding
